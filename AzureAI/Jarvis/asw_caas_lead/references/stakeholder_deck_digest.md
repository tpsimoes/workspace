- CSS Azure Strategic Workload Support Team
- Why ASW
- Who we are
- How we work
- When customers have mission-critical workloads on Azure, they expect Microsoft to provide a KnowME and end-to-end support experience, reducing their support effort with fast resolutions
- We are a dedicated team of 40 engineers and 5 managers focused on enhancing the workload support experience for our target customers (SAP & EPIC).
- Technical support, we provide Azure VM, NET, Storage, Linux, and Backup support to reduce customer communication efforts and gather feedback for Azure IaaS feature enhancements.
- Proactive engagement, we actively build connections with CSI, ACE, CSAM, and CSA to advocate for customers on Azure platform capabilities and stability enhancements.
- A dedicated team possesses Azure Compute, Networking, Storage, Linux, and Backup support capabilities, along with industry workload knowledge. We understand the complex footprint of workloads on Azure and actively collaborate with Engineering and the Customer Success Unit to improve Azure support and deliver a quality experience through customer outreach.
- Reactive support: Demonstrates technical intensity and quickly resolves/mitigates technical problems.
- Learn the Customer: Understand the complex footprint of workloads on Azure and share support insights.
- Self-Help and Monitoring: Develop support diagnostic tools and dashboards.

## Slide 15
- Deliverable examples of Customer Outreach
- Grafana Dashboards
- The CaaS Lead Grafana Dashboards are crucial for both our reactive and, especially, proactive support efforts. They provide a centralized view of customer pain points, allowing us to analyze key data and trends effectively.
- By leveraging these insights, we can identify potential issues before they escalate and enhance our overall support strategy. This enables us to deliver more informed, data-driven solutions tailored to the customer's needs.
- These dashboards offer macro visibility into the customer's infrastructure, but it's also possible  to manage and track support tickets from the customer.
- By leveraging this data, we can drive more effective and informed support strategies.
