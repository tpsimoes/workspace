# Customer Introduction
Nike is an S500 strategic customer with Microsoft. Nike is a multinational corporation that is engaged in the design, development, manufacturing, and worldwide marketing and sales of footwear, apparel, equipment, accessories and services. The company is headquartered near Beaverton, Oregon. It is one of the world's largest suppliers of athletic shoes and apparel and a major manufacturer of sports equipment. Nike markets its products under its own brand and subsidiaries including Brand Jordan, Hurley International and Converse. Operating segments for the Nike brand are: North America, Europe, Middle East and Africa, Greater China, Asia-Pacific and Latin America.
