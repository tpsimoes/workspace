# Customer Introduction
[Medline](http://www.medline.com) - is the largest provider of medical-surgical products and supply chain solutions serving all points of care. 
Main business from 7:00 Am Cst to 7:00 Pm Cst. ​
Medline have a significant loss of two million per hour. This loss is quite substantial and needs to be addressed promptly. SAP ECC (MDP) is there heart of the system. ﻿​
EAST US is Primary and secondary region. HA is in place within the region under Zone 2. DR is not in place.

[Mission Critical Services for Azure Workload (MCSAW)](https://microsoft.sharepoint.com/teams/CSS/SitePages/Kardia-Program.aspx)​ is an evolution of Support for Mission Critical (SfMC) aimed at elevating enterprise customer's support experience for specific business critical workloads by improving the workload's reliability on Microsoft's Cloud platform and minimizing time to mitigation. ​
Medline is our first MCSAW customer running SAP workload on Azure.
