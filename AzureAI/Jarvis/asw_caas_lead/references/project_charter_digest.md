- FY25 & FY26 H1 Insight & Learning

> **Notes:** FY25 & FY26 H1 Lesson LearnStakeholder Engagement with CaaS : Not all stakeholders treat CaaS as a valuable resource. While CaaS Leads follow program guidelines and utilize the CaaS Lead Toolbox to build connections and drive engagement, some stakeholders show limited interest. This may be due to their workloads operating smoothly with minimal issues or a lack of interest in Mission Critical offerings.Microsoft Stakeholders Instability: Recent changes on Microsoft Organization had some impact on some of the Microsoft Stakeholders that usuaslity collaborate with the CaaS Leads, such CSAMs and CSA’s. Lack of Shared Best Practices Among CaaS Leads: CaaS Lead experiences and best practices have not been consistently shared, resulting in varied approaches. Although we have strong support engagement stories, each CaaS Lead applies their own methods. We have not established an environment that encourages sharing of insights to influence and inspire peers, nor have we facilitated the exchange of customer support tips, tools, and expectations across the ASW team.Mission Critical Customers and Top Accounts: Mission Critical customers do not always align with our top accounts. Not all native customers express interest in Mission Critical services, while some non-native customers purchase Mission Critical for SoA purposes—for example, Medline and Sealed Air.

## Slide 26
- BHP – M832 series Slow Performance Issue - Context
- Background
- BHP migrated to M832 VMs expecting better performance than M416 VMs; However, data replication time increased from ~2 hours (M416) to ~ 4-6+ hours on M832 (Backup/Recovery)??
- The M832 series is a flagship VM line specifically designed for high-performance workloads. It offers significantly more powerful capabilities than the M416 series, along with a higher cost.

| SKU | M832ixs_v2 | M416s_v2 |
|---|---|---|
| CPU | vCPU 832, Intel® Platinum 8280 (Cascade Lake) | vCPU 416, Intel® Xeon® Platinum 8180M 2.5GHz (Skylake) |
| Memory | Memory_GiB 23088 (24TB) | Memory_GiB 5700 |
| Network Card | NIC  Mellanox ConnectX-5 Adapter | NIC Mellanox ConnectX-4 Lx Ethernet Adapter |
| Cost | > ~$80/hr | ~$40/hr |
| Replication Time (East US<-> West US) | ~ 4-6+ hrs | ~ 2 hrs |

- Business Concerns
- The extended replication duration does not meet the backup SLA requirements for BHP's SAP/HANA environment.
- BHP invested in VM M832 but encountered lower performance compared to the legacy M416, which has affected their confidence in our flagship virtual machine offering.
- If customer decide to rollback from M832 to M416, Microsoft will not lose the ACR but the Trusted Cloud Platform service provide in from of BHP
- Investigation Roadblocks
- BHP SAP Operation Team
- BHP’s SAP operations are outsourced to a third-party provider (Accenture), creating a strong dependency on external vendors for issue diagnosis and resolution.
- Integration support is matter
- When operating in a highly complex SAP landscape, lack of technical ownership on the customer side can significantly slow down issue resolution, as it becomes harder to navigate across layers and pinpoint where the problem lies.
- Azure VM M832 become the issue
- BHP operations believed that switching to M832 caused the performance issues, and this shook their confidence in the premium-priced M832 series
- Vendor Teams
- BHP operation team had already engaged SAP and Red Hat vendor
- => No issue found
- Azure VM investigation focus on Compute, NET, Storage
- => No issue found
- Each vendor only analyzed their own layer
